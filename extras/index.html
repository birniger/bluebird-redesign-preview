From your hotel to the slopes

Staying in Lucerne? For full-day lessons your instructor meets you at your hotel and travels to the mountain and back with you: by train, with help on every connection, or by private taxi.

  1. Your hotel Lucerne · 436 m · we meet you here
  2. Train or taxi · 45 min
  3. Engelberg 1,000 m · the village
  4. Walk · 8 min
  5. Titlis valley station 1,050 m · the lesson starts
  6. Gondola · 15 min
  7. Trübsee 1,800 m · the first slopes
  8. Gondola & Rotair
  9. Titlis 3,020 m · the glacier

Ticket support

Your passes, bought for you

We buy your lift passes and train tickets for the day and send you a payment link before the lesson: at cost, with no fee. Not sure which pass you need? It’s on Plan your day.
