Renaming or retiring an answer

A lesson remembers which answer was chosen, not its wording. Renaming an answer changes it on every lesson booked with it, and its translation moves with it: use it to correct or improve the wording. For a different kind of lesson, add a new answer and retire the old one: the wizard stops offering it, and lessons booked with it keep it.

Wording

LatePoint wording

Replaces a few dozen of LatePoint's phrases where this add-on has its own wording, such as "Verschieben" for "Reschedule", "Bestellung" for "Auftrag" and "Your verification code" for "Your OTP Code", and writes German dates one way: "28. Nov. 2026", "28. November 2026". LatePoint's own translation stays underneath. Untick it to show LatePoint's wording and dates everywhere.

Swiss conventions

"ss" instead of "ß" in LatePoint's German, and times as "10:15–16:15" in every language, including the lesson descriptions in the wizard.

Email languages

A lesson's emails are in the lesson's language and a website order's in the language of its page. Each customer has a language too, set from the page of their latest online booking and shown in their LatePoint profile; it is used for the welcome email and for an order entered by staff. Each instructor chooses theirs on their LatePoint profile.

Instructor Availability

A popup calendar of the days an instructor is free, with their schedule, bookings and Google Calendar taken into account.

Option 1: Shortcode [bluebird_instructor_availability agent_id="1"]

Shows a "View Availability" button. Replace 1 with the instructor's ID from LatePoint → Agents.

Option 2: Custom Button

Add these custom attributes to any button or link:

Attribute Value Required
data-bluebird-availability Instructor ID (e.g., 1) Yes
data-agent-name Instructor name (e.g., John) Optional
href # For links

Example:
<a href="#" data-bluebird-availability="1" data-agent-name="John">Check Schedule</a>
