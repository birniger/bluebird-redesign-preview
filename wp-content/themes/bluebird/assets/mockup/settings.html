Instructor preferences

Rank instructors for lessons with certain wizard answers, always or on certain dates. Answers left at Any match every value.

Always

SportLevelLanguage RankingEnforce

Instructor ranking

1 is asked first; several instructors may share a number. Instructors without a rank come next, then “last choice”.

On dates

FromTo SportLevelLanguage RankingEnforce
  • 1, 2, … — asked in this order; several instructors may share a number. Instructors without a rank come next, “last choice” after them.
  • Enforce off — the lesson keeps as few instructors and handovers as possible; the ranking decides between such plans.
  • Enforce on — the ranking decides every day, even if the lesson then needs more instructors or handovers. “Last choice” enforced means: only if nobody else is free.
  • When rules overlap, each instructor’s value comes from the best-fitting rule that names them: a dated rule before an always rule, then the one with more answers, then the shorter range. Dated rules that are over disappear.

Review Requests

After a lesson, LatePoint's "Booking Ended" automation asks the customer how it went. These limit how often one customer is asked. A single customer or booking can be left out in LatePoint: open the customer, or the booking in its order.

days

A customer who was asked in the last this-many days is not asked again. 0 means no pause.

Only Once

Money

A lesson cancelled before the free cancellation deadline (17:00 the day before, set above) comes off its order: the customer is told a refund is coming, or the open invoice is lowered. One cancelled later stays charged.

days before the first lesson

0 = on the day of the first lesson. If a lesson moves, the due date moves with it. Booked at shorter notice, the invoice is due straight away.

minutes

While a customer is paying, every day of their lesson stays held for this long, so nobody else can book it. A payment that arrives later still gets its lesson: a day taken in the meantime goes to another free instructor where the matching chose the first, or waits for a new date, and you and the customer are told.

Discount codes reduce

For every code in LatePoint → Coupons, partners' discounts included. A percentage applies to the lesson's price without its extras; a fixed amount takes off at most that price.

After a late cancellation

A lesson you cancel after the cancellation deadline stays charged. Ticked, the customer sees "Pay Balance" in their account; unticked, you collect it yourself.

For a different kind of lesson, add a new answer and retire the old one: the wizard stops offering it, and lessons booked with it keep it.

Wording

LatePoint wording

Replaces a few dozen of LatePoint's phrases where this add-on has its own wording, such as "Verschieben" for "Reschedule", "Bestellung" for "Auftrag" and "Your verification code" for "Your OTP Code", and writes German dates one way: "28. Swiss conventions

"ss" instead of "ß" in LatePoint's German, and times as "10:15–16:15" in every language, including the lesson descriptions in the wizard.

Instructor Availability

A popup calendar of the days an instructor is free, with their schedule, bookings and Google Calendar taken into account.

Option 1: Shortcode [bluebird_instructor_availability agent_id="1"]

Shows a "View Availability" button. Replace 1 with the instructor's ID from LatePoint → Agents.

Option 2: Custom Button

Add these custom attributes to any button or link:

Attribute Value Required
data-bluebird-availability Instructor ID (e.g., 1) Yes
data-agent-name Instructor name (e.g., John) Optional
href # For links

Example:
<a href="#" data-bluebird-availability="1" data-agent-name="John">Check Schedule</a>

School Calendar

The calendar for the whole school: a visitor picks the answers they know and a lesson, and sees the days on which at least one instructor who fits is free. A day opens to its times, each a link into the booking page with the answers and the day filled in.

Shortcode [bluebird_school_availability]

Put it on any page, in a Shortcode block. It works on a cached page.

Optional settings
Attribute What it does
booking_url The page a free time leads to. Empty: the page that holds the booking wizard, in the visitor's language.
contact_url Where a request-only lesson (a custom lesson) leads. Empty: the site's own request form if the theme names one, otherwise the booking page.

Example:
[bluebird_school_availability contact_url="/contact/#ask"]
