Founder · Snowboard instructor

Basil

I’ve been snowboarding since I was nine and taught for six seasons in Engelberg, Arosa and Gstaad before starting Bluebird.

What I enjoy most is seeing guests gain confidence, run by run. I keep lessons calm and structured: clear feedback, one step at a time, and a day planned around you, from the lift pass to the last run.

Not sure which lesson fits? Send me a message before you book; I usually answer the same day.

How I teach

Five things you can expect

  1. Calm, structured coachingClear feedback, step-by-step progress, no pressure.
  2. Confidence firstControl and comfort before speed or steeper terrain.
  3. Good flow on the dayWe adapt to the snow, your energy and what works.
  4. Practical helpRentals, timing and planning your day.
  5. Your goals, your styleTell me what matters and I shape the lesson around it.
